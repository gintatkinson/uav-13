## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS OVERVIEW

AVENGER 5 is an advanced, cost-efficient loitering munition designed for precision mid-range strike missions, delivering a CEP below 50 cm. Its low visual, acoustic, thermal and radar signatures improve survivability in contested environments, while its steep terminal approach supports effective engagement of targets in complex terrain.

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW AND BUNDLE OPTIONS

The platform is engineered for electronic-warfare resilience, featuring frequency-hopping communications and dead-reckoning navigation in GNSS-denied conditions. Targets can be detected and engaged using an EO/IR gimbal, visual tracking or geolocation references. Once the target is locked, AVENGER 5 can complete the engagement without continuous radio communication.

Its modular payload architecture supports UDS-provided thermobaric, HE and HEAT warheads, as well as integration of partner-selected payloads. AVENGER 5 is mechanically launched and can be integrated with SitaWare HQ and ATAK for coordinated mission execution.

Warhead availability, integration and use are subject to applicable regulations, export restrictions and end-user requirements. Performance depends on configuration, mission profile and operating conditions.

## KEY OPERATIONAL CAPABILITIES

[Figure: image spanning the lower half of the page]

[Figure: small graphic in the upper-right corner]

### Precision Strike Effect

Carries up to a 5 kg warhead, with HE, HEAT and thermobaric options. EO/IR guidance and visual target tracking enable engagement of stationary and moving targets, with a demonstrated CEP below 0.5 m against stationary targets.

### Extended Strike Reach

Up to 90 minutes of endurance provides the reach and time required to locate, confirm and engage targets beyond the immediate frontline.

### Autonomous Terminal Engagement

After target lock, AVENGER 5 can complete the terminal attack without continuous radio communication, increasing resilience against jamming and link disruption.

### Affordable Precision at Scale

A modular, production-ready strike platform combining interchangeable warheads, mobile launch capability and shared SwarmC2 mission software for scalable operational deployment.

*Depending on the mission's CONOPs (Concept of Operations), geolocation and weather conditions
