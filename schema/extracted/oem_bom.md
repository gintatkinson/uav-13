# OEM Bill of Materials (BOM)

The `Subsystem` column is also the AST part identity used by `ingest-sysml`. Rows are therefore one component per subsystem. `Not stated` means the value is absent from the six cited sources. All ESAD ICD rows carry `[prototype-reference] [Derived]`. The parachute row carries `configuration: non-mission` and `baseline: false`.
